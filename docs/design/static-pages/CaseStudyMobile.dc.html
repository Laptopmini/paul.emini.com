01

Context

The SmartThings Developer Console is the portal partners use to build, certify and market their products, from solo hobbyists to enterprise brands. It serves more than 120 partners and thousands of hobbyists, across 12 protocols such as Matter and Zigbee. Every paying partner goes through it to get certified.

02

The problem

Registering and certifying a product meant filling in one product form that had grown into dozens of fields, backed by several upstream services, each with its own validation and dependencies.

A change to a single requirement could block a partner from submitting anything else. Users were overwhelmed, and people dropped off out of frustration or confusion.

EVIDENCE

Hotjar, Google Analytics and the partner-success team all confirmed the drop-off.

03

The idea

I had already built the component library other Samsung teams were using, so I proposed consuming their work as versioned dependencies, under feature flags we controlled.

When our designer, Derek Warner, shared a Figma design for a new workflow with a summary, the two ideas came together. We called it the Unified Journey.

SEP 2025

A working vertical slice in a couple of daysBuilt on my own initiative while the roadmap was full through Q4.

04

How it works

  • Every section of a product is a self-contained module that provides a summary and a workflow.
  • A team that owns a domain we don’t can build and own its module, without us taking on their code or their on-call.
  • The app has only two generic pages, with no product-specific code.

05

The result

Partners establish what they’re building first, then only ever see the sections that apply: one overview with a completion status and their path to certification.

Impact

  • Every partner moved to it with zero breakage.
  • Fewer drop-offs, with more partners finishing.
  • Other teams build their own parts against the shared contract.
08 · What I’d do differently

Get it in front of real partner usage earlier, so edge cases surface before they hold up a release rather than during it.
